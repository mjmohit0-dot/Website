01 — CASE NOTES

The journey so far

I.

Underwriting the file, not just reading it

I've heard the "ye kis line mein aa gaye aap" question many times, and it's fair. I started by underwriting high-value loan applications as a Chartered Accountant. Every file taught me how credit decisions really get made — and where customers get stuck waiting.

II.

One question that wouldn't let go

How can technology make lending simpler, faster and better for customers? That question took me from the credit desk to digital product development at Piramal Finance. I'm genuinely grateful for the opportunity to learn and contribute in the digital space.

III.

Taking it a step further

I pushed the work forward by building AI capabilities into lending journeys — the foundation my product management career now stands on. Domain depth plus tech curiosity turned out to be a powerful combination.
